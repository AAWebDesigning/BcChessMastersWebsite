About BC Chess Masters

BC Chess Masters organizes CFC-rated chess tournaments in British Columbia for higher-rated players, played at classical time controls.

BC Chess Masters was founded with one goal: to help grow the chess community in British Columbia. We are a non-profit organization dedicated to supporting and developing chess across the province. BC is home to some of Canada's strongest chess players, and we want to provide them with more opportunities to compete, improve, and challenge themselves. Whether you're a titled player looking for strong competition, an aspiring master working toward norms, or a newer player aiming to climb the ranks, our tournaments are designed to help players of all levels reach their goals. Every dollar earned from our events is either reinvested into future tournaments or donated to organizations that directly support and strengthen the chess community.

What we do

  • Invitational events with a rating floor.
  • Classical time controls with increment.
  • CFC-rated results.
