Overview

BC Elite Invitational 1 is the first event from BC Chess Masters — a two-day, five-game tournament for players currently rated 2000+ CFC. Games are played at classical time controls with increment, the setting where real chess is decided.

The field is capped and registration runs on a first-come, first-served basis. Players rated 1900–1999 CFC may request a play-up into the field. Every game is CFC-rated.

Format & Eligibility

Time control
50 minutes + 10-second increment (Rounds 1, 2, 3) · 90 minutes + 30-second increment (Rounds 4, 5).
Eligibility
Open to players currently rated 2000+ CFC. Players rated 1900–1999 CFC may be granted a play-up by special request (a $20 play-up fee applies — see Entry Fee).
Rating
All games are CFC-rated. A current CFC membership is required to play — see the CFC Membership note below.
Forfeit time
Players will be forfeit after 30 minutes in the 50+10 format and after 60 minutes in the 90+30 format
Tie-break criteria
Standings ties are broken in the following order:
  1. Direct Encounter
  2. Buchholz Cut 1
  3. Buchholz
  4. Sonneborn-Berger
Standard Swiss tie-break order — adjust here if the organizer prefers a different set.

CFC Membership. Every player needs a current CFC membership to be rated. Join or renew at chess.ca/en/players/membership-join ↗ before the tournament.

Policies

Capacity

The field is capped at 25 players. Registration is first-come, first-served and closes when full.

Tournament policies

  • Fair play and CFC conduct rules apply throughout the event.
  • Electronic devices policy: All electronic devices are to be stored away. Devices found on person or riniging will result in a forfeit from the round and potential disqualification from the tournament at the arbiter's discretion
  • Byes: Up to 2 ½-point byes may be granted per player and must be requested beforehand. Byes for round 5 will be worth 0 points.
  • Default / forfeit time as published under Format.

Refund policy

Full refund on or before August 8th. 50% refund before August 27th. No refund after registration closes (August 28th).

Withdrawals

If a player wishes to withdraw from the event, notice must be given BEFORE rounds are paired. Once rounds have been paired withdrawls will not be considered.

Questions?

Reach out via the Contact page — including play-up requests for players rated 1900–1999.
